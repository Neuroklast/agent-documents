# Trust, privacy and ethical interaction

## Workflow and acceptance

- Make consequences, data use, optionality and current status understandable before decisions. Explain permissions in context and minimize collection.
- Provide neutral choices and comparable prominence for meaningful alternatives. Do not obscure cancellation, withdrawal or rejection.
- Avoid fabricated urgency, surprise charges, misleading defaults, confusing toggles and coercive retention. Do not optimize engagement over informed choice.
- Proportion confirmation, undo and recovery to risk. Mask sensitive values where appropriate; distinguish preview from committed changes.
- Define notification controls, frequency, quiet modes and recovery where applicable. Verify preference persistence and scope.
- Test denial, expiry, opt-out, export/deletion, role changes and repeated actions. Server authorization is mandatory.
- Output decision points, consequences, controls and evidence. See [auth](../../backend/auth.md), [security](../../security/security.md), [legal maintenance](../../legal/legal-maintenance.md); do not infer legal clearance.
