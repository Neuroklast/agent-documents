# Research and product discovery

## Workflow and acceptance

- Identify users, contexts, primary jobs, constraints, alternatives and failure costs. Separate observed evidence, reports, hypotheses and decisions.
- Inspect existing journeys, support issues and lawful analytics. Never invent interviews, personas, quotes or results.
- Choose observation/interviews for context, task testing for behavior, surveys for prevalence and controlled experiments for causal questions. Recruit representative users, including disabled users where relevant.
- Define neutral tasks with starting conditions and observable completion criteria. Record assistance, errors, recovery, time and abandonment without coaching participants.
- Prototype the riskiest assumption at the lowest useful fidelity. Compare alternatives using equivalent tasks. Protect participant privacy and minimize retention.
- Output: question, evidence table, prioritized findings, confidence, design implications and next validation step. A desk review is not user research. See [requirements clarity](../requirements-clarity/SKILL.md).
