# Visual design and art direction

## Workflow and acceptance

- Establish audience, content priority, desired character, existing brand and platform constraints. Describe concrete visual decisions, not adjectives alone.
- Design reading order before decoration. Use alignment, proximity, rhythm, whitespace and contrast to make the primary task recognizable.
- Establish a restrained typographic hierarchy, readable line length, appropriate line height and tabular figures for numeric alignment. Test real long text.
- Use an intentional grid and appropriate density; do not wrap every section in cards or force symmetry when content differs.
- Reserve semantic colors for meaningful roles; check themes and overlays via [accessibility](../../frontend/accessibility.md). Maintain consistent icon families and licensed imagery.
- Avoid gratuitous gradients, glass, glow, pills, hero blocks and oversized headings unless justified by the brief. Do not impose a universal aesthetic.
- Review actual screenshots at representative widths with real data. Output design direction, token decisions, compositions, exceptions and screenshot evidence. Token ownership remains in [design](../../frontend/design.md).
