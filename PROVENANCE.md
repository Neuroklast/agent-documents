# Provenance and distribution boundaries

The four native workflow skills use independently worded instructions for general software engineering practices. Historical inspiration: [BMad Method](https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD) (requirements and incremental delivery) and [Ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail) (complexity and risk review).

No upstream runtime, persona, hook, script or branded skill is included in these four replacements. No affiliation, endorsement or compatibility with either upstream product is claimed. Historical references remain in the change log for transparency.

Before distributing any collection or derivative, review its exact contents for ownership, copied expression, applicable third-party notices, and permission to distribute. This file is a scope statement, not a legal clearance or a grant of rights. Keep required third-party notices whenever applicable. The repository's existing distribution restrictions remain in effect.

Migration: replace the former four skill identifiers with requirements-clarity, incremental-delivery, evidence-review and complexity-review. Update explicit references and remove only obsolete copies you control. Existing downstream installations are not changed automatically.
